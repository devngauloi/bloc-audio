Bloc Focus

A focus timer with study music built in.

One full session a day, free. No ads. Nothing sells to you while the timer is running. There is no monthly plan.

Get Bloc Focus on Google Play

Android only for now. There is no iPhone version yet, and we would rather say so than let you find out at the App Store.

What it is

  • A 25-minute focus session with a 5-minute break, free every day, reset at midnight. It runs to the end — it is never cut short.
  • Four instrumental sounds. No talking.
  • Streak-shaming is off by default, and stays off.
  • No account, ever. Nothing here asks who you are. (A few anonymous product events go to PostHog in the EU — named in the privacy policy, not hidden.)

What it is not, honestly

  • No offline downloads — only the bundled sounds play without a connection.
  • The sound library is small.
  • The free tier is one session a day, at one length.
  • The music is AI-generated (instrumental, licensed for commercial use).

If you only want free study music, a YouTube playlist genuinely works and costs nothing. This is for people who want the timer and the quiet.

Premium

Longer sessions — 50/10, 90/15, or your own — and as many a day as you like. US$11.99 a year, or US$24.99 once. There is no monthly plan: the subscription people forget to cancel is the thing we decided not to sell. Cancel in one tap, from inside the app.
